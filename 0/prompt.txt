  ## Specific questions
  
  **(a) Concurrency and strings.**
  - Parsing is `par_iter` then a merge. DashMap is not on that hot path. In core it sits only in the DTS cache (`cache.rs`, `store: DashMap<CacheKey, SourceData>`) and in `WatchSession` (3 maps).
  - The binding's `SESSIONS` and tsc-client's `pending` are low-contention maps. A `Mutex<FxHashMap>` is enough for both. Replacing all four uses would remove dashmap and the hashbrown 0.14 duplicate (S effort, low risk, small gain: 1 crate, est. seconds of build time).
  - `papaya` and `scc` would only pay off with heavy concurrent writes. Here the pattern is collect-then-merge plus a small cache, so the benefit is unproven. Do not switch without a benchmark.
  - Keep arc-swap: `watch.rs` documents why `rcu` can't replace `Mutex<Arc<_>>` for the mutate-in-place case.
  - Keep rustc-hash. `ahash` has had no release since 2025-05.
  - compact_str vs `smol_str` 0.3.7 (active, O(1) clone via Arc for long strings): that could cut clone cost in GlobalSourceData merge. It is a 16-file churn and unmeasured, so benchmark first. Not recommended now.
  - Interning with `lasso` (last release 2024-08) or `internment` adds a global or per-thread interner. That contradicts the "no shared state, owned data" rule in `crates/core/AGENTS.md`. Skip.
  
  **(b) Cache format.**
  - `CACHE_SCHEMA_VERSION` is a manual constant (currently 4). ADR 0003 itself admits a forgotten bump decodes silently wrong.
  - postcard, bitcode and rkyv are also positional or layout-bound. They don't fix the risk, and rkyv is a large rewrite.
  - The fix is a guard, not a format swap:
    - (1) Add an insta golden test of `rmp_serde::to_vec` over a fully-populated `SourceData`, so any reorder or addition fails CI.
    - (2) Or mix the crate version or a field-name hash into the manifest.
  - Effort S, high value. Bitcode (about 3M downloads, 0.6.x) is not a clear speed win here (est.) and is optional.
  
  **(c) Errors.**
  - The split is consistent: core has no error crate, cli uses miette (30 hits), tsc-client uses thiserror 2.
  - No anyhow. Fine. The only stale item is miette's 18-month release gap.
