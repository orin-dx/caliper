PR #202 makes dependency loading the default, and 15 of its 16 checks pass. The one pending job is the non-blocking Equivalence check. The fork couldn't watch CI itself because its worktree was deleted partway through.

What changed
- Default: PipelineOptions::load_dependencies is true. The CLI has --no-load-dependencies on extract, inspect, watch and check, and --load-dependencies is still accepted as a no-op alias. The two flags conflict, and a flag overrides the config.
- Other surfaces: the NAPI, Vite plugin and addon docs and types say on by default, with loadDependencies: false to opt out.
- Docs: ADR 0019 (it supersedes 0010's "off by default"), the ADR index, README, STATUS, parity.md and a minor changeset.
- Pinned to the old behaviour on purpose: the corpus snapshot tests (with it on, the Mantine and Chakra snapshots changed because the installed csstype turns string props into literal unions), apps/validate/src/run-ours.ts, and scripts/parity.py, which passes --no-load-dependencies only to builds that know the flag.

The cost, plainly
- Chakra is 4.4× slower and holds 3.3× the memory by default. shadcn takes 40 ms and 124 MiB more. The other three take 10–20 ms and 16–25 MiB more.
- Against RDT's props (from the 0.4.0 baseline, not re-measured): Chakra 0.1% → 98.6%, shadcn 6.4% → 39.5%, antd 71.8% → 80.3%.
- Without node_modules: same props and unresolved count, 40 ms on against 30 ms off, no crash. Monorepos (Mantine, Chakra, Spectrum) ran without error.

Verified: the new default is byte-identical to the old build with --load-dependencies, and --no-load-dependencies is byte-identical to the old default, on all five corpora. Lint and 887 tests pass, plus the vite-plugin and addon tests.
